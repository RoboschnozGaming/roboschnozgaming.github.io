❔ How to Play
Enough to get you started — the rest is yours to discover.
The goal
Machines crawl the pipeline toward your reactor core. Every one that reaches it costs core integrity — bosses cost far more. Hit 0 and the run ends.
Building & upgrading
Tap a turret in the shop (its cost is shown), then tap a glowing panel to place it. Each turret type is good against different things — experiment to find what counters what.
Tap a placed turret to upgrade, move, or sell. Turrets climb to level 12 (higher with the ⭐ Mastery Overclock License, up to 15); every 4th level (4 / 8 / 12) unlocks a new ability. Selling refunds most of what you spent. Evolved towers climb to L20, gaining a bonus at 16 and again at 20 — plus their ⚡ Awakening signature if you've unlocked it in Mastery.
Scrap & the skill tree
Scrap comes from kills — and from starting a wave early with Next Wave ▶ (the ⚡ early-call bonus grows the more you chain it). Spend it on turrets and on the ⚛ Tech Tree.
The tree holds run-wide upgrades, one-time boosts, and capability unlocks. Junctions force a choice — taking one side locks out the other for the run. The tree resets each new game; 🛡️ Core Repair is pinned in the tree's top bar, next to your scrap.
Reading the battlefield
Some machines fly straight over the pipeline — only Gunner, Arc, Lance, and Flak can hit them, so always keep anti-air up. 🚀 Flak is the specialist: huge bonus damage to airborne targets, but 25% less against anything on the ground. Evolve it to Missile and it still hunts air first while hitting ground at full strength.
🎯 Targeting — tap a turret and cycle its target priority (First / Last / Closest / Strongest). At level 12 a Lance unlocks 🔒 Fixed Arc: lock it to a fixed bearing and it stops chasing targets, burning permanently along that line. Aim it straight down a long corridor and everything that walks the lane takes the full beam.
Two towers don't fight the usual way. 🧪 Corrosion does little direct damage but softens targets for your whole line — its acid burns over time, then (as it levels) marks targets to take extra damage, dissolves armor, and stacks that vulnerability higher; it's your answer to Juggernauts and other armored mobs. ♻️ Salvager deals no damage at all — instead it skims a cut of scrap from every kill within its radius, so plant it in the thick of your kill zone. Both can evolve: Corrosion → Meltdown (leaves corrosive pools), Salvager → Refinery (a bigger cut and reach).
Others don't just rush the core: a few will disable or damage your turrets, and many shrug off certain weapons while being weak to others. Watch what's marching in, and open 📖 Intel — it fills in details on each enemy as you meet it.
Bosses, surge & kits
Every few waves a boss arrives. Kill it (don't let it leak) for a ★ powerup — tap a turret to permanently boost it. After wave 20, a boss kill also opens the optional Bonus Surge: a brutal run on a new map for big scrap, where your towers redeploy and any upgrades you buy revert after.
Field Kits are rare one-shot tools in the left tray — tap to fire. They drop from boss kills and Surge rewards, carry between games, and you can raise how many of each you hold via ⭐ Mastery.
Power nodes, events & instability
⬡ Power-up nodes are special tiles on the board — build a turret on one to boost it. Types: Damage, Range, Rapid (fire rate), Overcharge (a little of everything), Shielded (immune to EMP, sappers & breakers), Bounty (extra scrap on its kills), Overload (chance to double a hit), Penetrator (ignores armor & shields) and Chill (briefly slows what it hits). Every run starts with one placed at random — decide which turret earns it. Earn more from mega-boss loot or the Power Node field kit, then tap an empty tile that has never held a turret to place it.
Events — after each mega-boss you're offered a choice that pairs a benefit with a price (e.g., faster fire rate but less scrap for a few waves). Accept takes both halves; Decline changes nothing.
⚠ Core Instability — when your core falls to half, and again to a quarter, the reactor AI offers two emergency protocols to choose between. Each pairs an upside with a downside and lasts the rest of the run. Short on good options? Spend scrap to reveal a third.

Between runs — lasting progress
⭐ Mastery (main menu) is your permanent progression. Every run earns XP — from kills, plus bonuses for bosses, treasure mobs and waves reached. Bosses also have a small chance to drop an XP Cache (100 / 500 XP, very rarely doubled). Spend it to permanently unlock tower evolutions, their ⚡ Awakenings (each evolved tower's signature power — a potent passive or on-kill effect that switches on once that tower reaches level 20), and run-wide upgrades — per-tower damage/range/fire-rate, Saturation burn duration & size, starting scrap and core, a head start, bigger field-kit capacity, and an extra starting power-up node — that apply to every future run.
🏆 Trophies (main menu) are permanent achievements that persist across all games and grant small lasting bonuses — separate from the per-run achievements that reset each game.
✦ Modifiers (main menu, before you start) let you opt into harder conditions — Glass Core (half core), Blitz (faster enemies), Fog of War (no wave preview), Austerity (less scrap), Overcrowded (more enemies) — each raising your score multiplier for that run.
🗓️ Daily (main menu) is a fixed daily challenge — one attempt per day on a set map, modifier set and enemy seed. It pays out Mastery XP and field kits, and your best Daily score is tracked.

🔒 Privacy Policy
Reactor Defense · Roboschnoz Gaming

Last updated: July 19, 2026

The short version: Reactor Defense has no accounts and no sign-up, and the game itself does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information.

Your game data stays on your device

Progress (Mastery, trophies, best scores, saved runs) and settings are stored locally on your device. We cannot see or access this data. It is deleted if you use "Reset all progress" in Settings or uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Google Play version shows optional rewarded video ads (only when you choose to watch one) and an occasional full-screen ad between games — never mid-wave. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may process device identifiers (such as your Advertising ID), IP address, and ad-interaction data under Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Where consent is required (EEA, UK, Switzerland, some US states) the game asks before any ad is shown, and you can change that choice any time via Settings → Privacy options. You can also limit ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Purchasing "Remove Ads" stops future ads.

Purchases

The "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing — we never receive your payment details and no third-party purchase service is involved. It is tied to your Google account so it can be restored after a reinstall.

Children

Reactor Defense is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data, run analytics trackers, or share information with third parties beyond the ad and billing services described above.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Questions: roboschozgaming@gmail.com
