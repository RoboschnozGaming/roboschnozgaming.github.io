About the game

Machines crawl a pipeline toward your reactor core, and every one that gets through costs core integrity. Place turrets on the open panels, upgrade them, and evolve them into stronger forms to stop each wave before it breaks through.

Reactor Defense: TD is a tower defense game built around long-term progression. Mastery XP carries across runs and unlocks new evolutions, while the enemy AI reads how you build and counters it. The whole game plays offline, with no account and no connection required.
