The short version: Reactor Defense has no accounts and no sign-up, and the game itself does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information.

Your game data stays on your device

Game progress (Mastery, trophies, best scores, saved runs) and settings are stored locally on your device. We cannot see or access this data. It is deleted if you use "Reset all progress" in the game's Settings or uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Google Play version of Reactor Defense shows ads in two ways: optional rewarded video ads, which play only when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward, and occasional full-screen ads between games (at most one every few new games, never mid-wave). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may process device identifiers (such as your Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location, and ad-interaction data under Google's privacy policya> and AdMob's data practicesa>. In regions where consent is required (the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and certain US states) the game asks for your choice before any ad is shown, using Google's consent framework; you can revisit that choice from the game's Settings. You can also limit ad personalization on your device under Android Settings → Google → Ads. Purchasing "Remove Ads" stops all future ads from being shown.

Purchases

The one in-app purchase ("Remove Ads") is processed entirely by Google Play Billing under Google's privacy policya> — we never receive your payment card or billing details, and no third-party purchase service is involved. Your purchase is tied to your Google account so it can be restored on a new device or after a reinstall; the game stores only a local "ads removed" flag.

Children

Reactor Defense is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service used by the app, contact us and we will help address it.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data, run analytics or tracking SDKs, or share information with third parties beyond the advertising and billing services described above.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
