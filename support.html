What to include in your email

  • Platform: Google Play, Apple App Store, or browser
  • Device model and OS version if you know them

Common questions

Lost progress? Progress is stored on the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing storage wipes it. If the game offers Export / Import in Settings, use Export before switching devices.

Remove Ads purchase not showing? Use Restore Purchases (App Store) or ensure you’re signed into the same Google account that bought it (Play). Then reopen the game.

No sound on iPhone? Check the silent / Ring switch and the in-game Sound toggle in Settings.

Ads? Rewarded ads are optional. Full-screen ads (when enabled) appear between games, never mid-wave. Remove Ads turns them off permanently for that store.
